description=Craft your own silent ranged weapons, make wooden or forged metal bolts from survival and workshop materials, and recover ammo from the dead when luck is on your side. No magic, no overpowered nonsense, no required books - just practical handmade weapons that fit naturally into the apocalypse.
description=
description=Includes:
description=• 4 crossbows: Crude, Improved, Compound, and Hand Crossbow
description=• Wood & Metal Bolt progression: knap stone heads in the wild, or forge/weld metal bolt heads (Blacksmithing 2 / Welding 3) for higher damage, crit chance, and durability
description=• Smart dual-ammo switching: fire both wood and metal bolts on every crossbow - press R to reload from inventory, quiver, or bags (auto-swaps bolt types when one runs out), hold R for the radial menu, or switch via right-click
description=• Crossbow repairs: fix worn crossbows in the field (tape, glue, epoxy), with a blowtorch, or at a forge
description=• Recoverable bolts with configurable break chances (broken metal bolts can be salvaged to reuse their metal heads)
description=• Bundled ammo: boxes of 10 Wood or Metal Bolts (full and short) - found as loot or packed by hand
description=• Bolt Quiver: a wearable pouch for wooden and metal bolts that grants +15% reload speed to all crossbows
description=• Custom models, textures, icons, and sounds
description=• Loot & foraging spawns with sandbox multipliers
description=• English and Ukrainian localization
description=• Singleplayer and multiplayer support
